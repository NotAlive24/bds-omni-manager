# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in BDS Omni Manager to be respectful, constructive and safe. Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Accept corrections and differing technical perspectives.
- Share reproducible information without exposing private server or player data.
- Keep discussions relevant and welcoming to people with different experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized content involving minors, deliberate exposure of credentials or private data, and repeated disruptive conduct.

## Maintainer response

Repository maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer using the contact method on their GitHub profile.

This policy applies to repository issues, discussions, pull requests and project-related communication spaces.
